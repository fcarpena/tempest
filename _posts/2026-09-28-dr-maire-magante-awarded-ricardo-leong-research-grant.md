We are pleased to share that Dr. Maire Carroline T. Magante, TEMPEST project partner and Chair of the Department of Economics at Ateneo de Manila University, has been awarded the Ricardo Leong Grant for Global Area Studies Research for the academic year 2026–2027. She is also the first Leong Institute Fellow from Ateneo’s Department of Economics.

Her study, “Climate Shocks and Internal Migration: Evidence from Typhoon Exposure in the Philippines,” will examine how typhoons affect internal migration. The research is undertaken in partnership with TEMPEST and Oslo Metropolitan University.

The Ricardo Leong Institute Fellowship Program supports the international mobility of Ateneo faculty, the development of geographical expertise outside the Philippines, and academic collaborations with partner institutions abroad. This award will strengthen the collaboration between Ateneo and OsloMet and support our joint research on the economic consequences of typhoons in the Philippines.

Dr. Magante joins this year’s recipients of the Ricardo Leong Grants and Leong Institute Fellows, whose projects represent a diverse range of research contributions to global and area studies.

Congratulations, Dr. Magante!
