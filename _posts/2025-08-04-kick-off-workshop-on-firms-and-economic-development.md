To kick off the project, we organized a one-day workshop on “Firms and Economic Development” last August 4, 2025 in Manila, Philippines.

The full workshop program can be found [here.]({{ '/assets/files/TEMPEST-Workshop-Program.pdf' | relative_url }})

Our partner institution, Ateneo de Manila University, hosted the workshop at Faber Hall, Loyola Heights campus.

The workshop featured keynote talks from Professor [Toshihiro Okubo](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=RCI63PsAAAAJ&hl=en) (Keio University) and Professor [Heiwai Tang](https://www.hkubs.hku.hk/people/heiwai-tang/) (Hong Kong University, Business School).

In addition, there were presentations by scholars from the World Bank, Central Bank of the Philippines (*Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas*), Seoul National University, the University of the Philippines, and Ateneo de Manila University.

More details about the workshop are available at the Ateneo de Manila University’s news page [here](https://www.ateneo.edu/news/2025/08/22/workshop-firms-economic-development-organized-tempest-typhoon-effects-enterprises), and more photos from the event can be found at their Facebook page [here](https://www.facebook.com/EcoDeptAteneoLS/posts/pfbid02jrFpsmycmzRPFXdS7yWxtmeHfxnmqVvEoSPgZFBF9f35c9oVknUFm7W9gvtRagMJl?rdid=KkwLankuQLiC5GzS).
